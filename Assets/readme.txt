Instructions:
1) Start the program
2) Select a path from the buttons at the top
3) Click Start
4) At the end, click the arrow which you think most accurately indicates your point of origin (where you started the walk)
5) Click restart

Data captured:
- Walk start/end
- Restarts
- Left/right footsteps
- When a knot in the road spline is reached (including its position)
- Allocentric/Egocentric arrow clicks
- Scene loads
